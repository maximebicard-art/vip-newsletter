CHANGE 1 — EVENTS: CRITICAL BUG FIX IN _add()
================================================================================

BUG (v3.8):
  EventsFetcher._add() was never checking whether the date was inside the
  allowed window (cutoff to lookahead). Every date from every source was
  accepted unconditionally. This caused:
  - Golden Globe Awards Jan 2027 appearing despite 120-day lookahead
  - Grammy Awards Feb 2027 appearing for the same reason
  - Sundance Film Festival May 28 2026 appearing (wrong date from homepage
    scraping, but passed because there was no check)

FIX:
  Added `if not (self.cutoff <= dt <= self.lookahead): return` as the first
  check in _add(). Now nothing outside the window can enter the list.

  Lookahead extended from 120 to 270 days (9 months) to properly capture
  the full awards cycle — Golden Globes (January) and Grammys (February)
  from the next calendar year will now appear correctly once they're the
  next upcoming edition.

CHANGE 2 — EVENTS: FILM FESTIVALS NOW USE WIKIPEDIA API
================================================================================

PROBLEM (v3.8):
  Homepage scraping found wrong dates for Sundance (May 28 instead of Jan),
  Venice (Jun 09 instead of Aug 27), and Telluride/Tribeca.
  Root cause: festival homepages contain many dates — press deadlines, sub-
  program dates, other Biennale events — that the generic regex picked up
  before the actual festival dates.

FIX:
  Replaced _fetch_film_festivals() with _fetch_film_festivals_wiki().
  Wikipedia uses a two-step API call per festival:
  1. Search Wikipedia for "2026 [Festival] Film Festival"
  2. Fetch the plain-text intro paragraph via the extracts API
  Wikipedia intros reliably state: "The [Nth] [Name] Film Festival was held
  from [date] to [date], [year]." — one clean sentence, no noise.

  The Wikipedia API is free, requires no key, and returns structured JSON.

CHANGE 3 — EVENTS: FASHION WEEK PROXIMITY EXTRACTION
================================================================================

PROBLEM (v3.8):
  BFC (London FW) returned May 30 — a press deadline on the homepage — instead
  of the September fashion week dates. FHCM (Paris FW) returned no dates.
  Root cause: generic "first date found on page" matched unrelated content.

FIX:
  Replaced _fetch_fashion_bodies() with _fetch_fashion_weeks().
  Dates are now only accepted if they appear within 300 characters of a
  season-specific proximity keyword:
  - Paris Men's FW: keywords = ["men", "homme", "menswear", "june", "juin", "ss27"]
  - Paris Haute Couture: keywords = ["haute couture", "couture", "july", "juillet"]
  - London FW: keywords = ["london fashion week", "september", "ss27"]
  - Milan Men's FW: keywords = ["fashion week", "june", "moda", "spring"]
  - NY FW: keywords = ["new york fashion week", "nyfw", "september"]
  - Paris Women's FW: keywords = ["women", "femme", "september", "septembre"]

  FHCM is fetched once and reused for all three Paris queries (Men's,
  Haute Couture, Women's) via a URL cache.

FashionUnited scraper: REMOVED. It caused the LFW May 30 false positive
in v3.8.

CHANGE 5 — AMBASSADORS: HYPEBEAST/HYPEBAE EXTRACTION FIX
================================================================================

PROBLEM (v3.8):
  Items from Hypebeast/Hypebae appeared with blank brands ("for ") because
  _extract_ambassador() didn't handle their headline formats:
  - "NINGNING Stars in Gucci's New 'Beauty and the Bag' Campaign"
  - "Jisoo for Salomon's Newest Global Ambassador Role"
  - "X Joins Y as New Global Ambassador"

  Also, Salomon, New Balance, and other sports-luxury brands were not in
  the brand recognition list, causing reversed talent/brand assignment.

FIX:
  Added three new regex patterns:
  1. "X Stars in / Featured in / Fronts Y's [adjective]"
  2. "X is Y's newest/latest ambassador/face"
  3. "X Joins/Becomes Y as New Global [role]"

  Expanded brand_words with: Salomon, New Balance, On Running, Hoka, Under
  Armour, Stone Island, Arc'teryx, Swarovski, Rimowa, Tumi, Montblanc,
  Moncler, Brunello Cucinelli.

  Added brand name cleanup: strips "'s New", "'s Latest", "'s Global",
  "'s Newest", "'s Campaign", etc. from extracted brand names so "Gucci's New"
  becomes "Gucci".

CHANGE 6 — INDUSTRY NEWS: FRENCH SIGNALS + LUXURY BRAND GATE
================================================================================

French language weak signals added:
  "crayons à lèvres", "maquillage", "make-up pour", "trésors make-up",
  "avant-première pour vogue", "prochains trésors", "à lèvres",
  "prochaines créations", "nouvelle création"
  These catch articles like the Pat McGrath/LV lip crayons piece (Vogue France).

Luxury brand gate for mid-market sources (Drapers, WWD):
  Articles from Drapers or WWD that mention NO luxury brand (LVMH, Kering,
  Hermès, Chanel, Dior, Gucci, etc.) AND are not M&A/regulatory are now
  rejected before the relevance scoring stage.
  This eliminates: Gap, American Eagle, Old Navy, Walmart, and similar mass-
  market earnings/strategy articles that have zero relevance for a VIP
  luxury/entertainment consultancy newsletter.

Additional weak signals added:
  "star-studded album", "inspired album" (music mis-filed as industry news)
  "french open fashion", "roland-garros" (sports fashion coverage)
  "where to watch", "now streaming" (guide articles)

CHANGE 7 — CASTINGS: NEW EXCLUDE SIGNALS
================================================================================

Distribution deals now excluded from castings:
  "buys rights", "acquires rights", "picks up rights", "distribution rights",
  "north american rights", "scoops up", "paramount buys", "buys starring"
  These catch: "Paramount Buys 'The Midnight Library' Starring Florence Pugh"

Festival industry programs excluded:

  "docfest", "film festival program", "industry program"

Where-to-watch guides excluded:
  "where to watch", "how to watch", "when to watch",
  "streaming on", "now streaming", "available on"
  These catch: "Where to Watch 'Criminal Minds: Evolution' Season 19"

SECTION REORDER — newsletter_template.html
================================================================================

The current section order in the template is:
  Key Events → Industry News → Ambassadors → Partnerships → Castings

For a VIP luxury/entertainment consultancy, ambassador and partnership
intelligence is the primary product — not broad industry news. Recommended:

  Key Events → Ambassadors → Partnerships → Industry News → Film/TV Projects → Castings

HOW TO FIX IN newsletter_template.html:
  Find the six `<!-- SECTION: ... -->` blocks and reorder them.
  The section blocks are identifiable by their <h2> section titles:
  1. "Key Award Shows & Events Ahead"     → stays first
  2. "Ambassador Announcements & Campaign Stars"  → move to position 2
  3. "Brand Partnerships"                 → move to position 3
  4. "Industry News"                      → move to position 4
  5. "Film Projects" / "TV Projects"      → position 5-6
  6. "Ones to Watch / Key Castings"       → move to last

  No Python changes needed — this is a template-only change.
